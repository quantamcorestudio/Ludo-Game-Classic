Player Trust & Safety

Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Quantum Core Studios is committed to player data privacy, transparent permissions, and safe gaming environments for Ludo Game Classic. Effective: November 2026.

Zero Data Selling Guarantee & Transparent Gaming

We believe in honest, player-first game development. Pass & Play and Smart AI modes are 100% offline and never transmit your board activity. Online features only use the minimal telemetry needed for fair competitive matchmaking.

Zero Data Selling 100% Offline Modes COPPA Compliant Encrypted Cloud Sync

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly necessary to provide seamless gameplay and online multiplayer services:

  • Profile Identity: Custom display name, chosen country flag, avatar, and optional player bio.
  • Gameplay Statistics: Matches played, win rates, dice roll counts, win streaks, and earned achievement trophies.
  • Device Telemetry: Device model, OS version, and screen resolution to optimize responsive rendering across phones and tablets.
  • Local Photo Cropping: When setting a custom profile picture, images are cropped locally on-device. We never inspect your private gallery.

2. Offline vs. Online Data Boundaries

We enforce strict architectural separation between offline and online features:

  • 100% Offline Modes: Pass & Play (Vs Friends) and Vs Smart AI execute entirely on your device. Zero analytics or gameplay packets leave your phone.
  • Online Esports Features: The International Arena and World Chat communicate securely via encrypted protocols solely to match you with global contenders and compute real-time ping (ms).

3. World Chat & Community Safety

Our dynamic World Chat connects thousands of players worldwide while keeping communications safe:

  • Public chat messages are broadcast in real-time to active channel participants.
  • Automated anti-spam, profanity, and harassment filters run actively to maintain a respectful, esports-ready environment.
  • Players can report or mute toxic profiles with one tap. Repeated violations result in automatic revocation of verified Blue Tick status and temporary chat bans.

4. Advertising & In-App Purchases

Monetization in Ludo Game Classic is transparent and non-intrusive:

  • Rewarded Ads: Optional rewarded video ads (e.g. for extra Lucky Wheel spins or bonus gold) are served via verified partners compliant with Google Play Families policy.
  • Zero Personal Profiling: We do not sell your personal gaming activity or contact details to third-party ad brokers.
  • In-App Purchases: Diamond and Gold bundles are processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing. Quantum Core Studios never receives or stores your credit card details.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Ludo Game Classic is a family board game enjoyed by players of all ages:

  • We comply strictly with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international standards.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 without verified parental consent.
  • Parents can play Pass & Play offline with children without creating any account or connecting to the internet.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You retain full rights and control over your player profile:

  • Cache Clearing: You can clear your local game data at any time via your device's Android settings.
  • Right to Erasure: To permanently delete your online sync profile, trophies, and leaderboard records, contact our privacy officer. All associated data will be erased within 30 days.
